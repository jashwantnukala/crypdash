## 📌 Project Overview

The cryptocurrency market generates enormous volumes of data every day — prices, volumes, market caps, and returns shift constantly across hundreds of coins. Analyzing such datasets manually is complex and often misleading without proper visualization.

This project focuses on **CRYPDASH 2020 – Crypto Market Analytics**, where raw historical cryptocurrency data is transformed into meaningful insights using **Data Analytics techniques**.

The project helps in understanding:

- Coin Performance & ROI 📈
- Market Phase Behaviour 🔄
- Risk vs. Return Analysis ⚖️
- Batting & Bowling Analysis ⚡
- Volatility & Market Cap Trends 📊
- Tournament Insights 🏆

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Cryptocurrency datasets are:

- Large and constantly changing
- Spread across many coins simultaneously
- Difficult to compare without visual context
- Hard to interpret for investment decisions

Important insights like:

- Which coins had the best return in 2020
- How market phases affected performance
- Which coins carry the most risk
- How volume and price correlated

often remain hidden in raw numbers.

This project solves this by creating a system that can:

✔ Simplify  
✔ Analyze  
✔ Visualize  
✔ Present data effectively

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Collect and organize historical crypto market data
- Clean and structure the dataset for analysis
- Identify KPIs like ROI, avg return, volatility, market cap
- Analyze individual coin performance across the full year
- Study market phases (Bull Run, COVID Crash, Recovery, Year-End Rally)
- Create an interactive web dashboard for better understanding

## 🗂 Dataset Description

### Dataset Used:
**CoinGecko Historical Cryptocurrency Dataset — Full Year 2020**

### Type:
**Daily OHLCV Dataset (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume)**

### Dataset Includes:

- Coin details (name, ticker, market cap)
- Daily price data (open, high, low, close)
- Volume and market capitalization
- Derived metrics (ROI, avg return, std deviation, volatility)
- Market phases (Bull Run, COVID Crash, Recovery, Year-End Rally)

This detailed dataset allows deep performance and risk analysis across 20 major cryptocurrencies.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

---

### 💰 ROI Analysis

- Calculated full-year ROI for all 20 coins
- Ranked coins from best to worst performer
- Identified top 5 performers: Chainlink, NEM, Cardano, Stellar, CRO

---

### ⚖️ Risk & Return Analysis

- Computed standard deviation and daily volatility per coin
- Classified coins into Low / Medium / High Risk
- Generated investment signals: Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Avoid

---

### 📅 Phase Analysis

- Divided 2020 into four market phases:
  - Bull Run (Jan – Feb)
  - COVID Crash (March)
  - Recovery (Apr – Aug)
  - Year-End Rally (Sep – Dec)

- Studied avg return, volume, and close price in each phase

---

### 📆 Monthly Analysis

- Tracked avg daily return per month
- Identified best and worst performing coin each month
- Compared total market volume across all 12 months

---

### 🪙 Coin Comparison

- Full side-by-side comparison of all 20 coins
- Metrics: ROI, avg return, std dev, volatility, avg close, year high, year low, market cap, risk level, signal

## 💡 Key Insights

### Major Findings:

- **Chainlink** delivered the highest ROI of the year at **9.98x**
- The **COVID Crash in March** caused the sharpest avg daily return drop (−0.55%)
- **Death Overs equivalent** — the Year-End Rally saw BTC hit its all-time high
- Coins with **lower volatility** (BTC, WBTC) offered more stable, predictable returns
- **Stablecoins** (Tether, USD Coin) had near-zero ROI — useful for risk analysis benchmarking
- Teams (coins) with higher strike rates (avg return) consistently outperformed
